Who can use the app

GSTPM Academy is for enrolled students. Your account is created by the academy when you enrol; there is no sign-up in the app. Use the same username and password as on the website.

Common questions

  • I can't sign in. Check your username and password, or use "Forgot password" on the website's sign-in page. If it still fails, email us your username (never your password).
  • A chapter is locked. Chapters open in order: finish the required lessons and pass the chapter quiz of the chapter before.
  • A video will not download. Downloads are set to Wi-Fi only by default; the app asks before using mobile data. You can download on up to two phones. Change this or remove a phone under Profile > Downloads.
  • A download stopped playing offline. Downloads must reconnect to the internet at least once a week to stay valid. Open the app online and they renew automatically.
  • My final exam does not open in the app. The final exam is taken on a computer through the website, in one sitting.
  • Where is my certificate? In the app under Results, and on the website. Anyone can check it with its certificate ID using "Verify online".

Reporting a problem

Email support@gstpm.in with your username, your phone model, what you were doing, and a screenshot if possible.
