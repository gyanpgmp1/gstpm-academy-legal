Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Applies to the GSTPM Academy website (app.gstpm.in) and the GSTPM Academy mobile app for Android and iPhone.

Who we are

GSTPM Academy is operated by Gyanendra Singh, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"). We provide project management training: courses, assessments and certificates. This policy explains what personal data we hold about learners, why, and the choices you have. For learner data we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

DataExamplesWhy
Account detailsName, username, email address, mobile number, batch or groupTo create and run your learner account, sign you in, and contact you about your courses

The mobile app does not contain advertising, does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data. It does not access your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Who we share it with

  • Your trainers and the academy staff who run your course, to support your learning.
  • Hosting and email providers we use to run the service (for example, to send password-reset and course emails), and our payment provider (Razorpay or Stripe) when you buy a course, only as needed and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Some lessons play videos hosted on YouTube or Vimeo; those players are provided by Google and Vimeo under their own privacy policies.
  • Anyone you give your certificate ID to can check that the certificate is genuine on our verification page, which shows your name, the course and the date.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How we protect it

  • Data is stored on servers in India. All traffic between the app or your browser and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes. The app keeps your sign-in token in the phone's secure storage.
  • Downloaded videos are encrypted on your phone, can only be played inside the app, and are removed when your access ends or you sign out.
  • Access to learner data is limited to staff who need it, and changes are recorded.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning records for as long as your learner account is active, so that your results and certificates can be verified, and afterwards only as long as the law requires (for example invoices under Indian tax rules). You can ask us to delete them earlier (see below). Sign-in tokens and downloads on your phone are deleted when you sign out. Server logs are kept for 90 days.

Your rights and choices, including deleting your account

  • See the data we hold about you, and correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data. Certificates already issued are then withdrawn and can no longer be verified.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, for example for optional emails.
  • Remove downloads at any time from Profile > Downloads in the app, and sign out to remove all downloads from the phone.
  • Complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the data protection authority where you live.

To use any of these rights, email support@gstpm.in from the email address on your account. We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 days and resolve them within 30 days. Accounts are created by the academy, not in the app; you can ask for deletion at the same address.

Children

Our courses are for working professionals and are not directed at children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
